Game Rules

Discover how the Bible fits together as one unified story through engaging, competitive gameplay rooted in sound theology and trusted scholarship.

Setup

  • 1
    Select either the Old Testament or New Testament deck.
  • 2
    Shuffle the cards well.
  • 3
    Deal 4 cards to each player.
  • 4
    Split the remaining cards into two draw piles (face-down) on the table.
  • 5
    The player to the left of the dealer starts.

Goal

The objective is to collect cards and create Bible theme sets which earn you points.

  • ★
    Each card belongs to a specific theme set, shown by the title at the top of the card (e.g. "Creation", "Conquest", "Passion Week", "Revelation").
  • ★
    Each card in a theme set counts as 1 point.
  • ★
    The first player to reach 20 points wins the game and shouts "Anchor!"

Theme Sets

Build sets from your hand and lay them on the table to score points.

  • 1
    After the first round, players may place available Theme Sets from their hand onto the table.
  • 2
    A Theme Set must contain a minimum of 4 cards from the same theme to be placed down for the first time.
  • 3
    Once placed, players may extend their Theme Sets during their turn (between actions) by adding one or more available cards.

Actions

Anchor's gameplay is inspired by
Matthew 7:7–8 (Ask, Seek, Knock).

Before playing together for the first time, we encourage you to read this passage in your Bible or by googling it.

On your turn, your first action is always to Ask. Based on the answer, you may need to Go Seek, and sometimes you'll want to Knock.

  • At the start of your turn, choose a player and ask them for cards from a specific theme set you are trying to collect. You must already have at least one card from the theme set you are asking for.
  • eg. "John, do you have any cards from the Exodus?"
  • If they do, they must hand over all matching cards to you.
  • If they don't, they say "Go Seek".
  • Draw one card from a draw pile on the table (choose which draw pile based on the back of the card you prefer).
  • Knock on the table and challenge another player! Select one card from their Theme Set you want to take, and ask theme a challenge question.
  • e.g. "Who was Abraham's father?" or “Did Jesus meet Nicodemus in the day or at night?”
  • If they answer wrong, you collect their card.
  • If they anwser correctly, they successfully defend their card.
Once per turn. Players may ask any question they can think of about the card, provided the information can be found in the Bible. If players disagree, check the reference passage(s) on the back of the card or google the passage(s) for the correct answer.

Additional Details

  • 1
    Transition cards (cards with two colours) can be used in theme sets of either colour.
  • 2
    If a you put down a Theme Set that includes a transition card, and that Theme Set already exists in another player’s collection on the table, you take their whole collection up to the end of that theme.
  • 3
    Once a Theme Set is placed down, it is no longer considered part of the player’s hand and cannot be retrieved.
  • 4
    A Theme Set remains on the table even if it is reduced below 4 cards by other players’ knocking actions.
