Start with a Game

One of the biggest barriers to discipleship knowing where to start, with Anchor it's simple! You don't need to know everything, we have done a lot of the hard theology for you.

  • Step 1: Pray and ask God to reveal who He has placed in your life already that you can help spiritually form. Often God works through the existing friendships and relationship we have.
  • Step 2: Organize an easy place to meet up one-on-one. Play a game of Anchor over coffee, tea or hot chocolate, and have some fun! Anchor has been made to be fun whether you know a lot or little about the Bible.
  • Step 3: As you play together, questions and moments of curiosity will surface on their own. Share what you know. Speak into what you notice. And when you don't know the answer, say so. Let it naturally drift into discipleship and when you are ready try one of the following:
  • Pick any card from the Anchor deck that catches their interest. Curiosity and guided discovery are powerful tools for learning. Work together to figure out where the card fits in the biblical timeline, then read part of it (or the whole card if it's shorter). Let the conversation naturally flow into real-life application. Afterwards, encourage them to continue exploring the deck until you meet again.
  • Choose a Theme Set you know well from the Anchor deck and walk through it together. Show how each card connects to the next, how the theme fits into the bigger story of Scripture, and share some of the lessons you have learned from it. Then encourage them to continue exploring the remaining cards on their own before you meet again, so they can share their insights and discoveries with you.

Spiritual Growth
Spiritual growth is becoming more like Christ — growing in love, wisdom, faith, and obedience over time. It is not measured in Bible knowledge alone, but in transformation: the gradual reshaping of how you think, speak, and live.
  • It is a lifelong journey, not a single moment.
  • It shows up in your relationships, character, and choices.
  • It requires all three elements of this formula working together.
Why multiplication? If any one element is zero, the whole equation is zero. You can hear without responding. You can respond without truly processing. Growth only happens when all three are active.
Hearing God
The Bible is the primary way God speaks to us; it is how we hear His voice. God is constantly speaking into every person’s life, whether that is calling someone to repentance and salvation for the first time, lovingly exposing disobedience, bringing healing to deep hurt, or many other things. God is speaking into our lives whether we are listening or not. The Seed is the area of spiritual growth that God is currently speaking into and inviting us to hear and respond to.
Processing
Once we have clarity on what we feel God is leading us into (what He is revealing), we can begin devoting time to truly understanding it. By digesting it, meditating on it, reading about it, and allowing it to move from head knowledge to heart knowledge, we are watering the seed God has planted.
Responding
After hearing what God is saying to us and taking time to process it, the final (and often skipped) step in our spiritual growth is responding. It is choosing to live differently after hearing and processing the truth God has revealed to us. This is the Fruit that is visible in our lives.

Applying this formula to discipleship is simple. When you meet with someone, whether over coffee, hot chocolate, or tea. Help them discover what God may be leading them uniquely into (the Seed). Ask good questions, listen well, and pay attention to where God is already at work in their life as you play the game and work through the cards.

Once the seed becomes clearer, help them process it (watering). Point them to Scripture, encourage reflection, pray with them and help them consider that this may be an area where God wants to grow them right now.

Then, after walking through it together and diving deeper over time, help them put it into action by responding to the revelation God has given them (Fruit).
