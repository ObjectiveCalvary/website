Guided Discovery

"And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he explained to them what was said in all the Scriptures concerning himself."
Luke 24:27

When we understand the context of the Bible passage(s) we are reading, we allow the Bible to speak for itself rather than reading our own ideas into it. This is where Anchor's guided discovery shines! Start anywhere and begin building up your mental story-map of the redemptive picture. Some helpful launch points:

  • 1
    Go in order of time: Start at the beginning of the Old or New Testament Anchor decks and work through it in order.
  • 2
    Explore a theme: Choose a theme like Kingship, Return, or Passion Week and understand how that theme shapes and fits into the redemptive plan
  • 3
    Random curiosity: Pick any of the cards that ignites your curiosity and go find out what happened.
  • 4
    Practice the order: Shuffle your cards, then put them back in sequence. Doing this alongside your reading builds up your biblical literacy.
📖 Use Anchor as a tool, not a rule. It exists to serve your journey into the Bible, not to define its boundaries. Go off-script whenever the Holy Spirit leads you there.
