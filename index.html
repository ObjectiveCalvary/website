About

Objective Calvary

• Objective Calvary, or OC, derives its name from two defining principles: being purpose-driven (“Objective”) and Gospel-focused (“Calvary”).

• OC’s innovative ideas equip the Church with Gospel Tools that help communicate the unchanging message of the Gospel effectively within a changing world. While times, cultures, and generations continue to change, the Good News of Jesus Christ never will.

• As the Church, we have a responsibility to continually seek new methods, approaches, and tools to communicate the life giving truth of Jesus Christ clearly and effectively to each new generation. We can not improve upon the Gospel; we seek better ways to communicate it.
