We Believe in

Kingdom Culture

The team of OC consultants are committed to a Kingdom Culture mindset. Believing in an unobstructed Gospel according to Paul’s writings in 1 Corinthians 9:12, and strive to ensure that the OC Gospel Tools reflect this conviction. Therefore, all digital Gospel Tools are distributed free of charge, while any physical or electronic products are offered at the lowest possible cost, covering only what is necessary to sustain their production, innovation and distribution. All OC consultants and contributors work with the desire to build into the Kingdom of God, rather than for personal financial reward.

We Believe in

Spirit-led Work

In Genesis 2:15, God instructs Adam to cultivate and care for the Garden. In Exodus 35:31–33, the Spirit of the Lord fills Bezalel with wisdom, understanding and skill to craft the Ark of the Covenant. Then, in Matthew 28:18–20, the resurrected Christ commissions His disciples to go and make disciples of all nations.

Throughout Scripture, we see that God chooses to co-labour with humanity, giving us responsibility and equipping us through His Spirit.

We believe in Spirit-led work. We recognise that God's wisdom, understanding, creativity and excellence surpass our own. We therefore depend on His inspiration and guidance in everything we do, seeking to work through His Spirit and do all things wholeheartedly, as unto the Lord (Colossians 3:23).

We Believe in

Relationship

Jesus teaches that loving the Lord our God and loving our neighbour as ourselves are the greatest commandments, on which all the Law and the Prophets depend (Matthew 22:37–40). From the beginning, God's desire has been for us to commune with Him and love one another.

We believe that work should never be cold or transactional, but deeply relational. We place genuine care, love and the desire to love one another at the forefront of everything we do, believing that how we work with people matters just as much as the work itself.
